Privacy

In short: this website sets no cookies, does no tracking or analytics and loads nothing from third parties.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you open it, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, the time, the page requested and your browser, in order to deliver the website and keep it secure. Nico has no access to this data. Data may be processed in the USA. Details are in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Legal basis: legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

No cookies, no tracking, no third parties

There are no cookies, analytics tools, advertising or social media plugins. All files, including the 3D library and the models, come from this website itself. Text uses your device's system font.

Language setting

If you switch the language, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (entry lang), so the website remembers it on your next visit. It is not a cookie, never leaves your device and can be deleted in your browser settings.

External links

Links to LinkedIn, Instagram, GitHub and other websites only take effect when you click them. From then on, the privacy policy of that website applies.

Contact by email

If you write to Nico, your message and email address are only used to reply to you.
